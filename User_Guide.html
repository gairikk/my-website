Guide for Booking Food Coupons, Accessing My Coupons, and Adding More Meals.

1. How to Book Food Coupons

  1. Open the App & Go to "Book" Tab: Start at the main booking interface.
  2. Enter Details & PIN (Step 1): Type your full name, choose your Block and Flat number. Set a secure 4-digit PIN.
    ⚠️ Important PIN Disclaimer: Remember this 4-digit PIN! You will need to enter this exact PIN in the "My Coupons" tab to view, access, or add more coupons later. Do not forget it. Check the data privacy agreement box and click Next.
  3. Select Meals (Step 2): Navigate through the Puja days (Saptami, Ashtami, etc.). Choose plate quantities for Breakfast, Lunch, or Dinner, choosing Veg/Non-Veg options and Dine-in vs. Parcel boxes (+₹15 charge). Click Next.
  4. Review & Pay (Step 3): Check your order summary. Select either Cash (pay at desk) or UPI (scan QR or pay via app and enter your 12-digit UTR reference number).
  5. Get Your QR Tickets: Confirm your booking. Your digital tickets with per-meal QR codes will generate instantly.

2. How to Access "My Coupons" & Book More Meals

  1. Switch to "My Coupons" Tab: Click the lookup tab at the top of the app.
  2. Enter Credentials: Enter your exact Full Name, Block, Flat Number, and your 4-digit Security PIN (the one you used during booking). In case of any issues with your PIN, please contact the Puja Committee Admin. Click Search Coupons.
  3. View by Day & Meal Tabs: Your booked meals appear organized by day tabs and meal buttons (Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner). Each meal card displays its specific QR code, plate breakdown (Dine-in vs Parcel), and real-time delivery status.
  4. Book More Coupons: Tap the "➕ Book More Coupons" button inside your ticket view or lookup result. This pre-fills your flat credentials and redirects you straight to Step 2 to add more meals without re-entering your details. Any new booking will merge under your existing Order ID.
💡 Payment Verification Note: If you paid via UPI or Cash, your QR codes will show as locked (⏳ Payment Pending) until an admin verifies the payment. Once verified, your QR codes unlock automatically.
