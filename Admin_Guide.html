🛡️ Bhog Bela — Operations Manual

Guide for Committee Admins, Counter Volunteers, and Kitchen Staff.

1. Committee Admin & Payment Verification (`🧾 Admin` Tab)

  1. Access with PIN: Enter the committee security PIN to unlock the admin panel.
  2. Bulk Subscription Upload: Upload your society's `paid_flats.csv` file using the upload box to automatically sync annual festival subscription clearance statuses into Firestore.
  3. Verify Pending Payments (Payment Bifurcation): Scroll down to Pending Payment Verifications and review orders. When residents book more coupons, the review screen splits the verification clearly:
    • Total Amount = Aggregated amount of all bookings combined.
    • Already Paid = Amount paid/verified for earlier bookings.
    • New Booking Amount = Amount to be verified now (paid in cash or via UPI to be verified via URN).
    Cross-check the URN/Cash receipt and click "Mark Payment Verified" to unlock new QR codes.
  4. Export Reports: Download the Master Master Report CSV or the Kitchen Prep Forecast CSV for cooks at any time.

2. Counter Volunteers / Food Service (`🍽️ Counter` Tab)

  1. Unlock Counter: Enter the volunteer access PIN.
  2. Scan Resident QR Codes: Click "Start Camera Scanner". Point your phone's camera at the resident's meal QR code. The app automatically validates the cryptographic HMAC signature, prevents duplicate scans for already-served plates, plays a confirmation beep, and logs the meal delivery live.
  3. Manual Lookup: Alternatively, type the resident's Order ID, Name, or Flat number in the search bar, review their precise Dine-in vs. Parcel plate breakdown, and tap "Serve 1 Meal".

3. Kitchen Staff Real-Time Feed (`👨‍🍳 Kitchen` Tab)

  1. Access Live Feed: Enter the volunteer/kitchen PIN.
  2. Live Preparation Dashboard: View real-time aggregated counts of verified plates to cook and parcel boxes to pack for each active meal slot (Veg vs. Non-Veg counters vs. parcel boxes). Tap "Refresh Kitchen Feed" to update numbers instantly as payments get verified by admins.
