📖 Bhog Bela Resident User Guide

Welcome residents of Magnolia Oxygen! Learn how to book food coupons, manage your security PIN, track your QR code delivery status, and add more meals seamlessly.

1. How to Book Food Coupons

  • Step 1 (Details): Enter your full name, select your Block and Flat number, and set a secure 4-digit PIN.
    ⚠️ Remember this PIN! You will need it to view your coupons or add more meals later.
  • Step 2 (Choose Meals): Select your plate quantities across Puja days (Saptami, Ashtami, etc.), choosing between Dine-in or Parcel boxes (+₹15 charge).
  • Step 3 (Review & Pay): Choose Cash or UPI payment. Complete the transaction or record your 12-digit UTR reference number.

2. How to Access "My Coupons" & Book More

  • Go to the "My Coupons" tab, enter your Name, Block, Flat, and your 4-digit PIN.
  • View your day-wise meal codes and live delivery tracking (e.g., 0/1 served).
  • Click "➕ Book More Coupons" to add more meals without re-entering your details. New bookings automatically merge under your existing Order ID.
